Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

FitMosaic respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use FitMosaic.

FitMosaic does not collect or store your personal data on external servers.

Information Stored on Your Device

FitMosaic stores your progress and app settings locally on your device, including:

  • Weight goals
  • Progress tracking data
  • Selected characters
  • Premium purchase status
  • App preferences

This information stays on your device and is not uploaded to external servers.

Crash Reporting

FitMosaic uses Bugsnag to help identify and fix app crashes and technical issues.

Bugsnag may collect limited technical information such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash logs and diagnostics

This information is used only to improve app stability and performance.

Photos Permission

If you choose to save progress snapshots, FitMosaic may request access to your photo library.

Photos are saved directly to your device and are not uploaded anywhere.

Advertising

The free version of FitMosaic may display non-personalized advertisements.

These ads are not based on your personal behavior or activity.

Advertising providers may still use limited technical information, such as device type or approximate region, to deliver and measure ads.

Children’s Privacy

FitMosaic is not directed toward children under 13 years old.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
